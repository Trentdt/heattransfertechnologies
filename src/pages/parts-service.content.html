Parts & Service

Equipment doesn't stop needing support once it's installed — and it doesn't matter whether we originally supplied it. Heat Transfer Technologies' parts and service team keeps cooling towers, closed loop coolers, heat exchangers, and chillers running at the performance they were designed for, for the life of the equipment.

Repair & Field Service

When equipment underperforms or fails, our field service team diagnoses the problem on-site and gets it fixed — from mechanical repairs to troubleshooting performance issues that datasheets alone won't explain. We support emergency repair calls as well as planned repair work scheduled around your outage windows.

Maintenance Contracts

Scheduled maintenance catches the small problems before they become unplanned downtime. We structure maintenance contracts around your equipment, duty cycle, and risk tolerance — from basic inspection programs to full-coverage agreements for critical cooling systems.

Performance Evaluations

If your cooling equipment isn't hitting the thermal performance it used to, a performance evaluation tells you why — fouling, mechanical wear, control issues, or a process change the system was never sized for. We evaluate equipment against original and current operating conditions and give you a clear read on repair, retrofit, or replacement.

Parts Sourcing

We source replacement parts across a broad manufacturer network, including for equipment we didn't originally supply. If you're not sure what part you need or where to get it, send us the equipment tag or nameplate information and we'll track it down.

Whether it's an emergency repair, a maintenance contract, or a part you need sourced, tell us what you're running and what's going on.
