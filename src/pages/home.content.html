Heat Transfer Technologies supplies, designs around, and services the cooling and heat transfer equipment that keeps industrial and power facilities running: open and closed cooling towers, closed loop coolers, air-cooled heat exchangers, chillers, filtration systems, and the pumps and controls that tie a cooling system together.
