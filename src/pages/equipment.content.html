Equipment

Heat Transfer Technologies supplies and specifies equipment across the full range of industrial cooling and heat transfer applications — matched to your process, water chemistry, footprint, and budget, and backed by our parts and service team for the life of the equipment.

Category

Cooling Towers

Open and closed circuit cooling towers for industrial and power applications, sized and specified around your heat load, water quality, and site constraints — from single-cell replacements to multi-cell installations for large process or power facilities.

 Category

Closed Loop Coolers

Closed circuit (fluid) coolers that combine the water savings of a closed loop with the heat rejection performance industrial processes require — protecting critical equipment from the fouling and scaling risk of an open loop.

 Category

Air-Cooled Heat Exchangers

Air-cooled heat exchangers for facilities where water use, water rights, or water treatment costs make air cooling the better long-term choice — engineered for the ambient conditions and duty cycle of your site.

 Category

Chillers

Chillers for process cooling and comfort cooling applications, specified around load profile, redundancy requirements, and refrigerant strategy — supplied and supported as part of the same relationship that covers your towers and heat exchangers.

 Category

Filtration Systems

Filtration equipment to protect downstream heat transfer equipment and extend its service life by keeping cooling water clean — the category that gets overlooked until fouling starts costing you thermal performance.

 Category

Pumps & Controls

The pumps, valves, instrumentation, and control systems that tie a cooling system together — sized and specified alongside the rest of your equipment so the whole system performs as designed.

Cooling Towers

Typical applications: condenser water cooling, process water cooling, HVAC and central plant cooling, power plant auxiliary cooling.

What we help with: thermal performance evaluation, replacement/retrofit specification, drift and plume mitigation options, water treatment coordination, and startup support.

Closed Loop Coolers

Typical applications: process fluid cooling, quench water, jacket water cooling, standby/emergency cooling loops, applications with strict water chemistry requirements.

What we help with: loop sizing, glycol/fluid selection guidance, and integration with existing plant piping and controls.

Air-Cooled Heat Exchangers

Typical applications: compressor and engine cooling, process gas cooling, oil coolers, power generation auxiliary systems, oil & gas and midstream facilities.

What we help with: fin/tube selection for your service, fan and drive selection, and noise and footprint considerations for site-constrained installations.

Chillers

Typical applications: process chilled water, plant utility loops, temperature-critical manufacturing processes, backup/redundant cooling capacity.

What we help with: load calculation support, redundancy and N+1 planning, and coordination with existing chilled water infrastructure.

Filtration Systems

Typical applications: cooling tower basin filtration, sidestream filtration, process water filtration ahead of heat exchangers and condensers.

What we help with: filtration technology selection matched to your water quality and fouling history, and integration with existing water treatment programs.

Pumps & Controls

Typical applications: condenser water and process water circulation, VFD-driven pumping for variable load systems, cooling tower and chiller plant control upgrades.

What we help with: pump selection and sizing, controls integration and upgrades, and system-level troubleshooting when performance doesn't match design.
