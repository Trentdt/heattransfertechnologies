Markets Served

Cooling and heat transfer equipment fails in different ways in different industries — and the cost of failure is never just the equipment. Heat Transfer Technologies works across the markets below with the process knowledge to specify equipment that fits the application, not just the duty point on a datasheet.

Power Steel Oil & Gas Process Hydrogen Carbon Capture

01 Power

Baseload, peaking, and combined-cycle power facilities depend on cooling systems that can't take unplanned downtime. We support power generation customers with condenser and auxiliary cooling equipment, performance troubleshooting, and the parts and service response that outage schedules demand.

02 Steel

Steel production runs some of the harshest water chemistry and thermal loads in industry. We specify and support cooling towers, closed loop coolers, and heat exchangers built to hold up to mill-side conditions, with service support that understands mill outage windows.

03 Oil & Gas

From upstream compression to midstream processing, oil & gas facilities rely on air-cooled heat exchangers, closed loop coolers, and process cooling equipment engineered for remote sites, variable ambient conditions, and strict uptime requirements.

04 Process

Chemical, industrial gas, and general process manufacturing facilities need cooling equipment matched precisely to process fluid, temperature, and purity requirements. We support process customers from initial equipment selection through the filtration and controls that keep systems performing to spec.

05 Hydrogen

As hydrogen production scales from pilot to commercial facilities, cooling system design is a first-order engineering decision, not an afterthought. We bring cooling tower, closed loop, and heat exchanger expertise to hydrogen projects moving from concept to construction.

06 Carbon Capture

Carbon capture facilities place major new thermal loads on a site's cooling infrastructure. We support carbon capture projects with the equipment specification and supply experience to integrate new cooling capacity into existing or greenfield sites.
